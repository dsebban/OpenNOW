# Qt update-phase experiment

The candidate is `QT_QPA_UPDATE_IDLE_TIME=0`, with vsync and the current threaded render loop unchanged. It targets delay between a GUI video update and Qt scene-graph synchronization. It is an experiment, not an established improvement. Source line references below use revision `6873b8c`, before the additive census instrumentation.

Fresh XRandR/root-window evidence confirms 3440×1440 at 60.00 Hz under GNOME Shell/X11: one primary DisplayPort monitor, identity transform, current/preferred 319.890 MHz mode and a 797×334 mm monitor size. This supersedes the earlier reduced 1720×1440 result; screenshot dimensions are not display-mode evidence. Ten one-Hz GPU samples observed active graphics clocks of 200, 400, 1875 and 1900 MHz, memory clocks of 800 and 1200 MHz, and GPU busy at 22–23%. Manual policy remained active. These snapshots do not prove a fixed 200 MHz cap or measure the video decoder's clock.

The installed `chimeraos-device-quirks-git` rule at `/usr/lib/udev/rules.d/30-amdgpu-power-profile.rules` sets manual policy with profile 1, 3D_FULL_SCREEN; live profile readback matches. Kernel documentation defines manual as permitting enabled-state and transition-heuristic selection, rather than forcing the lowest clock. A power-policy change is therefore not justified solely by one low-clock snapshot. [AMDGPU documentation](https://docs.kernel.org/gpu/amdgpu/thermal.html).

OpenNOW's `opennow-qt/src/streaming/NativeStreamRuntime.cpp:793` coalesces native notifications into a queued GUI drain. `StreamVideoItem.cpp:490` then calls the item's `update()`. In the exact Qt 6.9.1 source, `QSGThreadedRenderLoop::maybeUpdate` calls `postUpdateRequest`, which calls `QWindow::requestUpdate`. That update event follows vsync where supported, otherwise a fallback idle delay of up to 5 ms. The zero-idle environment override is documented for benchmarking. At a 16.67 ms frame period, this delay may move a ready source past a presentation boundary. [Qt 6.9.1 source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/qt/qtdeclarative/v6.9.1/src/quick/scenegraph/qsgthreadedrenderloop.cpp), [QWindow scheduling](https://doc.qt.io/archives/qt-6.9/qwindow.html), [Qt 6.9 scene graph](https://doc.qt.io/archives/qt-6.9/qtquick-visualcanvas-scenegraph.html).

Existing submit-to-swap timing starts after native preparation/composition recording at `opennow-qt/src/streaming/rendering/NativeStreamRenderCallback.cpp:491`. Its sub-1 ms p95 does not measure the earlier notification/update wait. The new observer should separate all-window swaps from fresh-source swaps and record first-pending-notification-to-GUI delay. Window swaps without a fresh source are not automatically repeated-video frames. Qt's `frameSwapped` marks presentation being queued, not physical scanout. [QQuickWindow signals](https://doc.qt.io/archives/qt-6.9/qquickwindow.html).

Calibrate the additive observer first. Compare unset versus zero-idle with the same seat, stream profile, display, window/overlay state and queues, using repeated scored windows and a closing control. Require fewer late fresh-source intervals beyond control spread while preserving the frozen FPS, latency, stream-ping and recovery gates. Examine socket drops alongside them. CPU distributions remain descriptive because collector batching can distort them. The override may be a no-op on a vsync-synchronized platform, or may increase wakeups and input contention. More redraws alone do not count as smoother fresh video.

Keep `QSG_NO_VSYNC` for a separately labeled diagnostic. Qt documents that it can change threaded pacing to system timers and may alter tearing/workload. The event-driven publisher hypothesis targets an earlier stage and should be tested separately, not combined with the idle override before either has evidence. No runtime environment or power policy was changed during this review.
